Setup checklist

      

      
Each step ticks itself off when the mailer can see it's done. Tap a step for instructions.

      
    
    
    What happens every cycle
    
      
    
        
    1SendEach sending inbox writes a short, ordinary email to one of your other inboxes or a seed. Plain text, no links, within today's limit.
    
        
    2LandThe receiving inbox is checked. Landing in the inbox counts toward placement. Anything in spam is moved back to the inbox.
    
        
    3EngageThe email is opened, marked important and sometimes starred. These are the signals that tell Google and Microsoft the mail is wanted.
    
        
    4ReplyAbout a third get a reply after a random, human-looking delay, so real back-and-forth threads build up.
    
      
    
    
    Your warm-up plan
    Per sending inbox, from its own day 1. Change the numbers under Settings, Ramp.
    
    
    What the colours mean
    
      ReadySet up and waiting for you to press Start warm-up.
      Warming upAll good. The inbox is sending on its ramp.
      Slowed downPlacement dipped below the slowdown line, so volume is halved until it recovers. Nothing to do.
      PausedYou paused it, or a safety rule did. The reason is shown on the inbox. Fix that first, then press Resume.
      BlockedIt can't send: DNS is missing for its domain, or no app password is saved.
      SeedReceives and replies only. Used to see how outside providers treat your mail.
    
    
    
    Your daily two-minute check
      
      
    1. Open Overview. The top banner should be green.
      2. 
      
    2. Inbox placement should stay at 90% or more. A few days below that is the moment to slow down, not speed up.
      3. 
      
    3. Open Replies once campaigns run, and answer real people there or in Gmail.
      4. 
      
    4. If an inbox paused itself, read the reason on the inbox before pressing Resume.
      5. 
    
    
    
    What it does every few minutes
    
      
      
        
    1. Reads every inbox and finds warm-up emails. Any it finds in spam are moved back to the inbox, and every one is marked read and important. Some get starred.
      2. 
        
    2. Replies to some of them after a random, human-looking delay, so conversations form threads.
      3. 
        
    3. Sends a few new plain-text emails between your inboxes, spread across working hours, following the ramp.
      4. 
      
    
    
    
    
    What protects your domains
    
      
      
        
    • The engine starts paused. Nothing is sent until you press Start.
      • 
        
    • DNS gate: a domain sends only when SPF, DKIM and DMARC are all found.
      • 
        
    • Hard limits: no more than 50 emails per sender per day, and the ramp adds no more than 5 per day.
      • 
        
    • Slowdown: when a sender's inbox placement over the last 3 days falls below the slowdown line, its volume halves.
      • 
        
    • Auto-pause: an inbox pauses if placement falls below the auto-pause line, if the provider refuses a send (spam, rate limit, blocked), or if 3 sends fail in a row.
      • 
        
    • Clean content: plain text only, with no links, images or tracking. Links are stripped even when the AI writes one.
      • 
        
    • Working hours only, with lower volume on weekends.
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    Campaigns
    
      
      
        
    • An inbox joins a campaign only after its warm-up has earned it. By default that's 14 days and 90% placement, and it never sends more than the cold cap a day.
      • 
        
    • Emails go out one at a time inside the campaign's hours, with a random gap between them. Follow-ups reply in the same thread.
      • 
        
    • A reply stops that person's sequence and keeps them out of every other campaign. Bounces, unsubscribes and "remove me" replies go on the do-not-email list.
      • 
        
    • An inbox pauses itself if more than 5% of its recent campaign emails bounce.
      • 
        
    • A lead with an unfilled variable is held back, so nobody gets "Hi {{first_name}}".
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    When to start cold email
    
      
    Start cold email from an inbox only after about 14 days, and only once its placement has held at 90% or more for 7 days. Begin at 10 to 15 cold emails per inbox per day and keep the warm-up running alongside. Never send cold email from these warm-up inboxes faster than the ramp they've built.
