What it does every few minutes

      
    
        
  1. Reads every inbox and finds warm-up emails. Any it finds in spam are moved back to the inbox, and every one is marked read and important. Some get starred.
    2. 
        
  2. Replies to some of them after a random, human-looking delay, so conversations form threads.
    3. 
        
  3. Sends a few new plain-text emails between your inboxes, spread across working hours, following the ramp.
    4. 
      

    

    
What protects your domains

      
    
        
  • The engine starts paused. Nothing is sent until you press Start.
    • 
        
  • DNS gate: a domain sends only when SPF, DKIM and DMARC are all found.
    • 
        
  • Hard limits: no more than 50 emails per sender per day, and the ramp adds no more than 5 per day.
    • 
        
  • Slowdown: when a sender's inbox placement over the last 3 days falls below the slowdown line, its volume halves.
    • 
        
  • Auto-pause: an inbox pauses if placement falls below the auto-pause line, if the provider refuses a send (spam, rate limit, blocked), or if 3 sends fail in a row.
    • 
        
  • Clean content: plain text only, with no links, images or tracking. Links are stripped even when the AI writes one.
    • 
        
  • Working hours only, with lower volume on weekends.
    • 
      

    

    
When to start cold email

      
Start cold email from an inbox only after about 14 days, and only once its placement has held at 90% or more for 7 days. Begin at 10 to 15 cold emails per inbox per day and keep the warm-up running alongside. Never send cold email from these warm-up inboxes faster than the ramp they've built.
